Understanding the Issues

SHE KNOWS is a platform for learning about issues affecting women and girls around the world.

💗 Why SHE KNOWS?

Because knowing matters.

Knowing what is happening. Knowing the facts. Knowing your rights. Knowing that your voice matters.

SHE KNOWS is a space to learn about issues affecting women and girls, understand the reality behind the headlines, discover stories history forgot, and hear voices that deserve to be heard.

Learn. Understand. Speak up. 💗
