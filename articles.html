Sexual Violence • Murder • Conviction

Kolkata R.G. Kar Medical College case

A junior doctor was raped and murdered at R.G. Kar Medical College and Hospital in Kolkata in August 2024. The case sparked major protests by doctors and widespread national attention over women's safety and hospital security.

📍 Location: Kolkata, West Bengal, India
📅 Incident: August 2024

👮 Investigation: India's federal investigative agency investigated the case. The prosecution relied on circumstantial evidence and examined 51 of 128 listed witnesses during the trial.

📢 Public attention: Junior doctors held demonstrations and work stoppages demanding justice and better security in hospitals.

⚖️ Court decision: Sanjay Roy was convicted in January 2025 and sentenced to life imprisonment.

Legal status: Conviction and life sentence reported. The defence said it intended to appeal.
Court decision reported: January 2025

Source: Reuters

🇳🇵 Nepal — Crime Reports

Child • Sexual Violence • Court

Garima Chaudhary case reaches Bara District Court

The case involving the death of 12-year-old Garima Chaudhary in Simara, Bara, generated widespread public outrage and demands for justice. The case later reached the Bara District Court.

📍 Location: Simara, Bara, Nepal
📅 Court order: September 2026

👮 Investigation: Police investigated the case and filed charges against multiple accused people.

📢 Public attention: The case led to widespread public outrage and discussions about justice and protection of children.

⚖️ Court action: The Bara District Court ordered two juveniles to a child correction home pending trial.

💰 Compensation: The court ordered Rs. 200,000 in interim compensation for the victim's family.

Legal status: Proceedings were ordered to continue; this was not a final conviction.

Sources: Onlinekhabar | The Himalayan Times

Harvey Weinstein — New York criminal case

The Harvey Weinstein case became one of the most closely followed sexual-assault prosecutions in the United States and generated extensive discussion about accountability and sexual misconduct.

📍 Location: New York, USA

⚖️ Court history: Weinstein's original New York conviction was overturned in 2024. Later proceedings produced another conviction in the New York case.

📢 Public attention: The case received international media coverage and became closely connected with broader discussions about sexual misconduct and accountability.

Legal status: The case has involved multiple trials and appeals; always check the latest court record before treating any earlier decision as final.
