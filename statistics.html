🌍 Understanding Crime Against Women & Girls

Crimes affecting women and girls take many different forms. They can happen in homes, schools, workplaces, public spaces and online. This page brings together statistics from organizations such as WHO, UN Women, UNICEF, UNODC and the ILO.

Important: The percentages on this page do not represent one single worldwide "percentage of all crime." Each statistic measures a specific crime, harmful practice, population or study. The source and population are shown with each statistic.

🚨 Major Crimes & Harmful Practices

⚠️

Sexual Violence

8.4%

Women aged 15–49 worldwide who experienced non-partner sexual violence at least once since age 15.

Intimate-Partner Violence

Ever-partnered women aged 15–49 who experienced physical and/or sexual violence from a current or former male intimate partner.

🕯️

Partner / Family Killings

60%

Around 60% of women and girls intentionally killed in 2023 were killed by intimate partners or other family members.

UNODC / UN Women — 2023
🔗

Human Trafficking

61%

Women and girls represented 61% of detected trafficking victims in 2022.

UNODC — Global Report on Trafficking in Persons 2024
👧

Child Marriage

19%

About 19% of young women aged 20–24 worldwide were married or entered a union before age 18.

UNICEF — global estimate
🛑

Female Genital Mutilation

230M+

More than 230 million girls and women alive today have undergone female genital mutilation in the countries where population data exist.

WHO / UNICEF — 2024–2026 estimates
🏢

Workplace Sexual Violence & Harassment

8.2%

Women in employment who reported experiencing sexual violence and harassment at work during their working lives.

ILO — Global Survey, 2022

Online Violence

16–58%

Studies estimate that between 16% and 58% of women worldwide have experienced some form of technology-facilitated gender-based violence.

UN Women — 2025 summary of available studies
🚶‍♀️

Public-Space Sexual Harassment

50–100%

Studies from different settings have found that 50–100% of women experienced sexual harassment or other forms of sexual violence in public spaces.

UN Women — studies across different settings

🕯️ Women & Girls Killed in 2023

85,000

Approximately 85,000 women and girls were intentionally killed worldwide in 2023.

Around 51,000 — about 60% — were killed by an intimate partner or other family member.

👤 Who Commits These Crimes?

❤️ Current or Former Partner

WHO estimates that 25.8% of ever-partnered women aged 15–49 have experienced physical and/or sexual violence from a current or former male intimate partner.

👨‍👩‍👧 Family Members

In 2023, around 60% of women and girls who were intentionally killed were killed by an intimate partner or another family member.

👤 Non-Partners

WHO/UNODC estimate that 8.4% of women aged 15–49 experienced non-partner sexual violence since age 15.

🔗 Traffickers

Women and girls represented 61% of detected victims of human trafficking in 2022.

📍 Where Can These Crimes Happen?

Crime against women and girls can occur in many different environments. The available international statistics do not provide one percentage for every location, so each figure below is labelled by its study.

🏠 Home / Private Spaces

Intimate-partner violence is one of the most widely measured forms of violence against women.

25.8%

Ever-partnered women aged 15–49 experiencing physical and/or sexual violence from a partner.

WHO — global 2023 estimate

🏢 Workplace

8.2%

Women in employment experiencing sexual violence and harassment at work during their working lives.

ILO — global survey

🚍 Public Transport

91%

Women and girls in a Quito baseline study who experienced some form of violence on public transport.

UN Women — Quito, Ecuador. NOT a worldwide percentage.

🌳 Public Spaces

50–100%

Studies in different settings have reported sexual harassment and other sexual violence affecting 50–100% of women.

UN Women — multiple settings. NOT one worldwide percentage.

📱 Online

16–58%

Available studies estimate that 16–58% of women have experienced technology-facilitated gender-based violence.

UN Women — global evidence review

🏫 School / Around School

50%

UNICEF reports that about half of the world's adolescents experience peer violence in and around school.

UNICEF — global estimate
